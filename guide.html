1 What the PBCC Live Queue is

A single, always-up order board for Palm Beach Card Co's TikTok Shop lives.

When you go live on TikTok, buyers place orders through TikTok Shop. The PBCC Live Queue polls the TikTok Shop Order API and turns every incoming order into a line on your board, automatically, in real time. Your job is simple: work the board from the top down, pull each buyer's cards, print a label, and mark them fulfilled.

The board does the fiddly parts for you:

  • It merges a buyer's orders into one slot, so a buyer who keeps ordering shows up once, not ten times.
  • It floats priority items to the top (breaks, slabs, whatever you configure).
  • It warns you about cancellations, holds, repeat buyers, and same-name buyers before you seal a bag.
  • It keeps a running 🐷 Piggy Bank tally of specific variants sold, a public queue for viewers, an optional Discord mirror, and a full CSV export.
Everything runs on Eastern timePBCC operates on Eastern (EST/EDT). Every time you see or type in this app — stream start, clip times — is Eastern.

2 Getting into the admin

There are two doors:

This guide + sandbox
Opened with the shared guide password. Safe to hand to a helper — it does not unlock the live control panel. The practice Sandbox uses the same password.
The control panel
Lives at a private, unlisted admin URL. Getting in needs the panel password and, if two-factor is turned on, a 6-digit code from your authenticator app (or a one-time backup code). This is the door you drive the live from.
  1. Open the admin URL (it is unlisted — bookmark it; it is never linked from the public site).
  2. Enter the panel password. If prompted, add your 6-digit 2FA code.
  3. You land on the live control panel. A login lasts 30 days on that device, so you usually only do this once.
First time? Practice hereThe Sandbox is a full fake copy of the board with make-believe orders. Click everything — you cannot break anything and nothing is real. It is the fastest way to learn the flow before a live.

3 Going live

The board is session-based: nothing enters the queue until you say so.

When the top-left status reads OFFLINE (red dot), incoming TikTok orders are ignored — they do not queue. That is on purpose, so orders from a past stream or a test don't pile up.

  1. Start your TikTok live as normal.
  2. In the panel, hit ● Go Live. Confirm the prompt.
  3. The dot turns green, the badge flips to LIVE, and the board starts accepting orders. Anything left over from a stream you forgot to end is safely archived to Past streams first, then the board is cleared for a fresh session.
Go Live clears the boardGoing live starts a brand-new session and empties the active queue. Only do it when you are actually starting a stream. (Your Piggy Bank totals are cumulative and are not reset by Go Live.)

4 Reading the board

Top of the Active queue = the next buyer to handle.

Each row is one buyer's slot — all of that buyer's still-open orders combined. From left to right a row shows:

Position #
Where they sit in line. #1 is next.
Buyer name
Their TikTok display name, followed by any flag badges (see the next section) and their order number(s).
Items line
A quick summary of what's in the bag, e.g. 2× Pokemon 151 pack, 1× Lorcana booster. Same-name items are summed.
Time in queue
A live timer showing how long the buyer has been waiting.
Total
The combined dollar value of the slot.
Actions
The caret (expand), Label, Bump, and Fulfilled buttons.

The four stat tiles up top show, at a glance: In queue, Priority count, Queue value, and Fulfilled this stream. Below the active queue are collapsible Fulfilled this stream and Cancelled this stream sections.

How the order is decided

The board sorts itself automatically, top to bottom:

  1. A slot you manually bumped sits at the very top (only one at a time).
  2. Then priority slots — earliest priority purchase first.
  3. Then everyone else — earliest order first.

5 Flags & badges — what each means and what to do

The badges are your early-warning system. Read them before you seal a bag.

BadgeMeansWhat to do
PRIORITY The slot contains a configured priority item (a break, slab, etc.). It has floated to the top. Handle it ahead of normal orders — that's why it's up top.
3 orders This buyer placed multiple orders that were merged into one slot. Pull everything in the slot into one bag. Expand the row to see each order.
＋ ADDED MORE The buyer ordered again while they were already #1 — i.e. while you may have started packing them. Stop and add the new item(s) to the bag in progress. The ×2 / ×3 shows how many times it happened.
🔁 2 earlier today You already fulfilled other order(s) from this buyer earlier this same stream. Decide whether to combine shipments. It's a heads-up, not an error.
⚠ SAME NAME Two different buyers in the queue share the exact same display name. These are different people. Use the order number and the id … tag (and the username in the expanded view) to keep them apart. Never merge them.
⚠ CANCEL REQ A cancellation was requested — on the whole order or a specific item. Do not ship until you expand the row and pull the exact item(s) marked CANCEL REQUESTED. See the expanded view.
⏸ ON HOLD — verify TikTok put the order on hold for some other reason (address check, order combine/split, sample/exchange, pending review). Not necessarily a cancellation. Verify the order on TikTok before shipping.
Same name vs. same personSame-name buyers are keyed on their unique buyer ID, not their name — so the board knows two "cardfiend"s are two people even when TikTok shows the same handle. When merging happens, it only ever merges the same buyer.

6 The fulfill loop

This is the rhythm you'll repeat all stream.

  1. Look at #1. Read any badges.
  2. If there's a CANCEL REQ or ON HOLD badge, expand the row first and deal with it before anything else.
  3. Pull the buyer's cards. If it's a multi-order slot, pull everything in it.
  4. Hit 🏷 Label to print the 2.25×1.25 shipping label.
  5. Bag it, apply the label.
  6. Hit ✓ Fulfilled. The slot drops into Fulfilled this stream and #2 becomes the new #1.
  7. Repeat.
Fulfilled the wrong one?Open the Fulfilled this stream section and hit ↩ Undo on that buyer to drop the order back into the active queue. (If that buyer already has a newer order waiting, it can't auto-merge — the app will tell you.)

7 The action buttons

✓ Fulfilled
Closes the whole slot as done. This is your one-tap "shipped it" button.
▲ Bump
Force this slot to the very top, above priority. Only one slot can be bumped at a time — bumping a new one un-bumps the old. (It doesn't show on the row that's already #1.)
🏷 Label
Prints a 2.25×1.25 thermal label with the buyer's name, order number(s), a batch count if they have several orders, and an "EARLIER ORDERS TODAY" flag when relevant.
✕ Remove
Takes the slot out of the queue without fulfilling it — use it when a buyer cancels. It moves to Cancelled this stream (you can Restore it), not to Fulfilled.
▸ Caret
Expands the row to the full item-by-item view (next section).
Reset
Clears every active and fulfilled order for the current stream (priority words are kept). Rarely needed — use with care, it can't be undone.

8 The expanded order view

Hit the ▸ caret on any row to open its full detail.

The expanded view is where you confirm exactly what to pull. It shows:

  • Every item, grouped by order number — so a multi-order slot is broken out clearly. Anything the buyer added after they hit #1 is separated under a ＋ Added since top of queue heading.
  • Per-item cancellations. If a buyer cancelled just one line, that item is marked ⚠ CANCEL REQUESTED right in the list. Pull only what wasn't cancelled — leave the cancelled item out of the bag.
  • The buyer's username cross-reference. The board row shows the buyer's display name; the expanded view reveals their actual TikTok username. This is how you tell two same-name buyers apart. (If TikTok didn't provide it, it says so.)
  • The full, un-shortened order number(s) for looking the order up on TikTok.
CANCEL REQ = expand before you shipA red CANCEL REQ badge means something in that slot is cancelled. Always expand it and pull the exact flagged item(s) before sealing. If the whole order is cancelled, use ✕ Remove.

9 The Piggy Bank counter 🐷

A running, cumulative tally of specific variants sold.

The Piggy Bank is a per-variant sales counter that lives in a card on the admin page and is also shown on the public queue. For each tracked variant, it adds +1 per unit when an order reaches the top of the queue — so it counts real, worked-through sales, not just anything that lands.

Working with it

  • It counts automatically. You normally don't touch it during a live.
  • Counts are cumulative and persist across streams — Go Live does not reset them. They only change from a sale, a manual Save, or a Reset.
  • To correct a number: type the right value in the variant's box and hit Save.
  • To zero one out: hit Reset.
  • Every manual change is logged. Open ▾ Reset / edit log to see the before→after of each edit, so if a counter is ever changed by mistake the old number is recoverable.
Never call it anything elseOn PBCC this tracker is the Piggy Bank (🐷). That's the name buyers see on the public page too.

10 Stream Clips (editor aid)

One-tap timestamps so your video editor can find the good moments.

The 🎬 Stream Clips card lets you flag moments during the live. It's independent of the queue — purely a timestamp tool.

  1. Set the stream start time first. Enter the time the stream started in Eastern (EST/EDT), or hit Use now. Elapsed times are measured from this.
  2. During the stream, hit ✂ Time Clip whenever something worth clipping happens. Each tap drops a mark at the current moment.
  3. Each clip row shows its Eastern clock time and its elapsed time (h:mm:ss) since the stream start, plus a note field.
  4. Both are adjustable: edit a clip's elapsed value to nudge its exact moment (its Eastern clock time updates to match), and type a note so the editor knows what it is.
  5. When you're done, hit ⧉ Copy list to copy the whole clip list (start time, each elapsed + clock time + note) to paste to your editor. Clear all wipes the list.
The card also shows a live "running for" clockOnce the start time is set, the header ticks up so you always know how long you've been live.

11 Page size & order-# search

Page size
The Show __ per page dropdown above the active queue sets how many slots show at once (10 / 25 / 50 / 100). Handy when a busy stream stacks up a long line. Your choice is remembered on that device.
Search
The search box filters the queue by buyer name OR order number — full or just the last few digits. Great for jumping straight to a buyer who's asking about their spot in chat.

12 Discord mirror & mod commands

Optional — an auto-updating queue message in your Discord.

When enabled, the bot keeps a single pinned message in a channel that mirrors the queue (position, name, priority/bumped marker only — no totals, items, or personal details, exactly like the public page). Mods can drive it without opening the web app:

/queue
Show / refresh the live queue message.
/fulfill
Mark the top slot fulfilled — or add a buyer name to fulfill a specific slot.
/bump
Bump a named buyer to the top.

/fulfill and /bump are limited to mods (Manage Messages permission). The web dashboard is always the primary control surface; Discord just mirrors it.

13 Wrapping up (End Live)

  1. Work the queue down as far as you can.
  2. Hit ■ End Live. Confirm.
  3. The whole stream — fulfilled, cancelled, and any still-unfulfilled orders — is archived to Past streams, the board clears, and new orders stop entering.

If you still had orders in the queue when you ended, the confirm prompt tells you how many — they're archived too, so nothing is lost. Review or export any past stream (as CSV) anytime from 🗂 Past streams.

Exports

⬇ Export CSV in the Fulfilled section downloads the current stream: a summary by buyer, all fulfilled orders in detail, cancelled orders, and anything left unfulfilled. Long order numbers are kept intact for spreadsheets.

Don't forget to End LiveIf you start the next stream without ending this one, Go Live will auto-archive the leftovers for you — but ending cleanly keeps your Past streams tidy.

14 Golden rules

Keep these five in your head
  1. Green dot before you sell — no Go Live, no orders.
  2. Always work from the top down.
  3. Red CANCEL REQ or ON HOLD → expand and verify before you ship.
  4. Same name is not the same person — check the username / order #.
  5. End Live when you're done so the stream archives cleanly.
